Privacy Policy for Shield-AdBlocker

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Privacy Summary: Shield-AdBlocker is designed to perform its core functionality locally in your browser. Shield does not sell personal information and does not transmit your browsing history, visited website addresses, or extension settings to the developer.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shield-AdBlocker Chrome extension ("Shield", "the extension", "we", "our", or "us") handles information while providing ad-blocking, tracker-blocking, cookie-banner handling, and per-site protection controls.

2. Information Shield Accesses

To provide its advertised functionality, Shield may access limited information related to the websites you visit. This may include:

  • The domain or URL of the current website, where required for per-site protection controls.
  • Page content or page elements when necessary to identify or hide advertisements, trackers, or cookie banners.
  • Extension settings, preferences, and allowlisted websites.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by Shield is used only to provide the extension's user-facing features, including:

  • Blocking advertisements and tracking requests.
  • Applying protection rules to websites.
  • Allowing users to pause or resume protection on specific websites.
  • Remembering user preferences and allowlisted websites.
  • Handling supported cookie banners and advertising elements.

4. Local Storage

Shield may store extension preferences and allowlisted domains locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage mechanisms, such as chrome.storage.local.

This locally stored information is used only to remember your selected settings and to operate the extension correctly.

5. Data Transmission

Shield does not transmit your browsing history, visited website addresses, page content, allowlisted domains, or extension preferences to the developer's servers.

Shield does not use its own remote analytics, advertising, or telemetry service to collect personal information from users.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

Shield does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Shield does not share browsing information or extension settings with third parties for advertising, profiling, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

7. Browsing Activity

Shield may process browsing-related information locally when necessary to perform its ad-blocking and privacy-protection features. This processing is limited to functionality that is directly requested and expected by the user.

8. Permissions

Shield requests Chrome extension permissions that are necessary for its functionality. Depending on the extension version, these permissions may include access required to apply network blocking rules, store user preferences, and operate protection features across websites.

Permissions are used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.

9. Children's Privacy

Shield does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Shield does not operate a developer-controlled data collection service, it does not intentionally create user profiles or collect personal information based on age.

10. Security

Shield is designed to minimize data collection and keep extension-related preferences on the user's device whenever possible. No method of software operation can guarantee absolute security, but Shield is designed to avoid unnecessary collection or transmission of personal information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Shield's functionality, permissions, or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Shield's privacy practices, please contact the developer using the support contact information provided on the Shield-AdBlocker Chrome Web Store listing.
